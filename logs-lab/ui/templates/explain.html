The metrics, decoded

Recall — of all real failed logins, how many did it catch? (0–1, higher is better)

Precision — when it says "suspicious", how often is it right? (0–1, higher is better)

F1 — one combined score balancing the two. 0 = useless, 1 = perfect.

ROC-AUC — how well the model orders logins from most to least suspicious. 0.5 = random, 1 = perfect.

Small numbers are the honest result of hunting rare failures without crying wolf — ~10% of logins fail, and most are typos, not attacks.

05

Logs-Lab vs the RBA model

side quest vs main project

The main RBA project works the same way but on a 31M-row real dataset — and it was handed a cheat sheet: a known-bad-IP blocklist. That shortcut, not better learning, is most of the score gap. Removed it and both models land in the same honest range.
